Under the Tree: a daily listening habit.

What began as a story shared to school friends grew into a recurring audio practice spanning Tamil short fiction, spiritual and epic traditions, self-development, global non-fiction, temples, children’s stories and more.

How it works

A reading-to-audio ritual.

The dossier describes a disciplined practice: read widely, make notes, verify difficult points, condense, narrate, distribute — then archive carefully to avoid repetition.

Read

Across several shelves

Tamil literary fiction, spiritual texts, epic literature, applied self-development, English non-fiction, temple traditions and children’s material.

Shape

10–20 minute listening

Sources describe material being condensed into approachable audio segments, often prepared for morning delivery.

Share

Multi-platform circulation

WhatsApp, Telegram, podcast platforms, Facebook and YouTube have all been part of the distribution ecosystem.

Series & strands

What listeners can encounter.

This is a representative map of documented strands rather than an exhaustive episode index.

Tamil literature

Author-centred storytelling

Balakumaran, Sujatha, Sivasankari, La Sa Ra, Thi. Janakiraman, Vannadhasan, Vannanilavan, Ku. Pa. Rajagopalan, Puthumaipithan, Dilip Kumar, Prabanjan, Jeyamohan and others appear across the documented repertoire.

Children

Under the Tree Kutties

Panchatantra-type material and serialized children’s literature such as Jeyamohan’s Pani Manithan, alongside later children’s authorship.

Spiritual

Long-form devotional series

Lalitha Sahasranamam, Ramana Maharshi and Aksharamanamalai, Ramayana-related material, Yogi Ramsuratkumar, Bhagavatham, Temples of India / Divyadesam and Kurukshetra.

Access & model

Ad-free in spirit, donation-supported in practice.

The research distinguishes between platform advertising and voluntary support. Profiles emphasize her reluctance to interrupt stories with ads; the official site now maintains a donation page to support maintenance, reach and operating costs.
